Inkwell is a daily practice sheet for people who count calligraphy strokes against a quota they set. You dip the nib to wet the ink, then stroke while it is wet so today's tally moves toward the sheet capacity.

• Today's sheet with a stroke quota (sheet capacity)
• Dip to open eight seconds of wet ink
• Stroke the sheet while Wet to write a counted stroke and move the tally
• A dry stroke is a bleed and does not count
• Peel lifts today's last counted stroke
• A day files as filled when the tally meets capacity
• Day readings Blank, Partial, and Filled
• Nib readings Dry, Wet, and Filled
• Calendar of filled and partial days by month
• Charts: Weekly stroke volume, Fill streak (Filled days in a row), and Dry bleeds (Dry strokes)
• History of Stroke and Bleed rows marked Counted or Miss
• Latest lines of recent counted strokes and dry bleeds
• Set Strokes on a full sheet and Save capacity
• Export marks
• Show the introduction again
• Erase every mark
• Contact Inkwell

A new local calendar day starts a new blank sheet. Everything stays on this device.
