This is the first release of Inkwell, a daily practice sheet for people who count calligraphy strokes against a quota they set.

• Today's sheet with Dip, Stroke the sheet, and Peel
• Dip opens eight seconds of wet ink
• A counted stroke moves the tally; a dry stroke is a bleed and does not count
• A day files as filled when the tally meets the sheet capacity
• Calendar of days marked Blank, Partial, or Filled
• Charts with Weekly stroke volume, Fill streak, and Dry bleeds
• History of Stroke and Bleed rows marked Counted or Miss
• Settings for Save capacity, Export marks, Show the introduction again, Erase every mark, and Contact Inkwell

A device install starts with a blank sheet after the introduction and a capacity of 8. Everything stays on this device.
